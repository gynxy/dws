\# Shadow Call Missions



\## Overview



Shadow Call Missions are one of the most important daily activities.



!\[Shadow Call](../assets/images/shadow-call.jpg)



\## Priority Rewards



\- Speedups

\- Resources

\- Enhancement materials



\## Recommended Strategy



1\. Complete highest rarity missions first

2\. Refresh low-quality missions

3\. Coordinate with alliance buffs
